Hamburguer

This classic grilled hamburger features a juicy, perfectly seasoned beef patty topped with melted cheese, fresh vegetables, and your favorite sauces, all layered inside a buttery, toasted bun.

Ingredients

For the Beef Patties

  • Ground beef:1 lb / 450g (80/20 blend is best for juiciness)
  • Salt:1 teaspoon
  • Black pepper:½ teaspoon
  • Garlic powder:½ teaspoon

For the Assembly & Toppings

  • Hamburger buns:4 pieces
  • Cheddar cheese:4 slices
  • Butter:2 tablespoons (softened)
  • Lettuce:4 fresh leaves
  • Tomato:1 large, sliced
  • Red onion:1 small, cut into thin rings
  • Condiments:Ketchup, mustard, or mayonnaise

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Shape the Patties:Divide the ground beef into 4 equal portions. Gently shape each portion into a ball, then flatten them into discs slightly wider than your burger buns.
  2. Make a Dimple:Press your thumb gently into the center of each patty to create a shallow indentation. This keeps the burgers from puffing up and shrinking into hockey pucks while cooking.
  3. Season Well:Just before cooking, generously season both sides of each patty with the salt, black pepper, and garlic powder.
  4. Toast the Buns:Spread the softened butter onto the cut sides of the buns. Place them face-down on a skillet or grill over medium heat for 1 to 2 minutes until golden brown. Set aside.
  5. Cook the Patties:Heat a cast-iron skillet or outdoor grill to high heat. Place the patties down and cook without moving them for 3 to 4 minutes until a deep, flavorful crust forms on the bottom.
  6. Melt the Cheese:Flip the patties over and immediately place a slice of cheddar cheese on top of each. Cook for another 2 to 3 minutes until the cheese is completely melted and the burger reaches your desired doneness.
  7. Assemble and Serve:Spread your preferred condiments on the bottom bun. Layer the lettuce, tomato slice, and red onion. Add the cheesy patty on top, cover with the top bun, and enjoy immediately!
