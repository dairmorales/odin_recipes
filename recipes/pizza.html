Pizza

This classic homemade pizza recipe features a crisp, golden crust made from scratch, topped with a savory tomato sauce and perfectly melted mozzarella cheese.

Ingredients

For the Pizza Dough

  • Warm water: 1 ½ cups (355 ml)
  • Active dry yeast:1 package (2 ¼ teaspoons)
  • Bread flour or All-purpose flour:3 ¾ cups (490g)
  • Extra virgin olive oil2 tablespoons
  • Salt:2 teaspoons
  • Sugar:1 teaspoons

For the Toppings

  • Pizza sauce: ½ cup
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese:2 cups
  • Olive oil:1 tablespoon (for brushing)
  • Your choice of toppings:Pepperoni, sliced peppers, mushrooms, or onions

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Activate the Yeast:In a large bowl, mix the warm water, sugar, and yeast. Let it sit for 5 to 10 minutes until it becomes frothy.
  2. Make the Dough:Add the flour, olive oil, and salt to the yeast mixture. Stir until a sticky dough forms. Turn the dough onto a floured surface and knead for 8 to 10 minutes until it is smooth and elastic.
  3. Let it Rise:Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it tightly, and let it rise in a warm spot for about 1 to 1.5 hours until it doubles in size.
  4. Preheat the Oven:Turn your oven up as high as it goes—ideally 450°F to 500°F (230°C to 260°C). If using a pizza stone, place it inside while the oven heats.
  5. Shape the Crust:Divide the risen dough into two equal portions to make two 10-12 inch pizzas. Flatten and stretch the dough out onto a piece of parchment paper or a baking sheet. Use your fingers to press small dimples into the surface to prevent large air bubbles.
  6. Add Toppings:Lightly brush the edges of the crust with olive oil. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough, then sprinkle the mozzarella cheese and your favorite toppings on top.
  7. Bake:Slide the pizza into the oven and bake for 12 to 15 minutes, or until the crust is deeply golden brown and the cheese is bubbling and melted. Slice hot and serve immediately!
