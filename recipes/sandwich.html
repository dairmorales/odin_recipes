Sandwich

This ultimate turkey and bacon club sandwich is layered with crispy bacon, tender turkey, fresh veggies, and a creamy herb mayo, all stacked between perfectly toasted slices of bread.

Ingredients

For the Sandwich

  • White or wheat bread:3 slices (for a classic double-decker look)
  • Sliced turkey breast:3–4 deli slices
  • Bacon:3 strips (cooked until crispy)
  • Cheddar or Swiss cheese:1 slice
  • Tomato:2 thin slices
  • Lettuce:1–2 crisp leaves

For the Spread

  • Mayonnaise:2 tablespoons
  • Dijon mustard:½ teaspoon (optional, for a little tang)
  • Garlic powder:A pinch

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Cook the Bacon:Fry the bacon strips in a skillet over medium heat until they are crispy. Transfer them to a paper towel-lined plate to drain off excess grease.
  2. Toast the Bread:Place all 3 slices of bread in a toaster or brown them lightly in a skillet with a little butter until golden and crisp.
  3. Mix the Spread:In a small bowl, stir together the mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, and a pinch of garlic powder until smooth.
  4. Build the First Layer:Lay out the first slice of toasted bread and spread a thin layer of the mayo mixture on top. Add the lettuce, tomato slices, and the crispy bacon strips.
  5. Build the Second Layer:Spread mayo on both sides of the second slice of bread and place it on top of the bacon. Layer the sliced turkey and the cheese on this middle piece of bread.
  6. Close the Sandwich:Spread the remaining mayo on one side of the third slice of bread and place it face-down on top of the cheese to close the sandwich.
  7. Cut and Serve:Press down gently on the sandwich. Insert 4 toothpicks halfway into each of the four sides, then use a sharp serrated knife to cut diagonally into 4 triangular quarters. Serve immediately!
